NUGENIS Inc. ("NUGENIS," "we," "us," or "our") operates the EPICVIVA website (the "Website"). EPICVIVA is a consumer brand of NUGENIS Inc.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Website, how we use it, and how we handle information submitted to us.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to keep the Website simple and collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described below.

Information You Provide to Us

If you contact us through the contact form on the EPICVIVA homepage, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as:

  • Your name

The Website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most websites, hosting and network infrastructure may process certain technical information associated with requests to the Website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, date and time of access, and requested pages.

We do not use the Website to create user accounts, process payments, or collect information for online purchases.

We may use information you provide to:

We do not use information submitted through the contact form to send marketing emails unless you separately provide consent or otherwise have a lawful basis for such communications.

3. Contact Form Service

Our contact form uses Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service, to receive and forward contact-form submissions to us.

When you submit the contact form, the information you enter is transmitted to Web3Forms for processing and delivery to us. Web3Forms states that submitted form information may be stored by the service for a limited period depending on the applicable Web3Forms plan.

Web3Forms currently states that form submissions are stored for 30 days on its free plans and for 1 year on its Pro plans. Its applicable terms and privacy practices may change over time.

You can review Web3Forms' current privacy and data-handling information on its website.

We recommend that you do not submit sensitive personal or health information through our contact form.
